## Judgement calls

Our reading rather than the teacher's instruction — check these against her if it matters:

- **The opening (lines 1–3).** She struck *my dad's freezing cold car,*, underlined *went* and *down*, inserted *I* before *went*, and replaced *to* with *prepared to*. The clean copy applies those literally ("Zooming into the park, I went down prepared to learn…"), which reads awkwardly; her remark *he needs to alight the car first before zooming into the park* says the order is to be rebuilt, and she did not write it in. The page says so. It is also unclear whether the child meant the car or themself to be zooming.
- **`in to` → `into`** (line 1), **the comma after *park***, **`what` → `What`** and **`my` → `My`** after the speech (lines 4–5): not marked by her. Added in the clean copy with a dotted green underline as ours. The *My* after `!"` and the *My* → *my* after *After that,* (line 29) are capitalisation consequences of her other marks.
- **Lines 19–20, `I saw I facing`.** She struck *saw* and wrote *was* above, which leaves two *I*s side by side (*Suddenly, I I was facing*). The clean copy drops the second. The page tells the child.
- **Line 15 punctuation.** She crossed out the mark after *(was gone)* with a red x and wrote nothing in its place. The clean copy has a full stop, dotted green, because *I started* begins a new sentence.
- **Line 16, `in sheer horror`.** The first word looks like *sneer* in pencil; read as *sheer* because she ticked it and *horror* continues on the next page.
- **Line 16, comma → `and`.** She wrote *and* below the line with a caret, not above; applied before *my jaw*.
- **`(sp)` on `grinned`.** The *(sp)* sits between lines 7 and 8, nearer *grined* than *hand*; read as belonging to *grined → grinned*, with *(T)* belonging to *hand → handed*.
- **The tag under `But` (line 10)** is unreadable. It might be a connector code (*Con*). The page treats it as the same `Start` mark as *However,* in Week 11 and says so.
- **The `(Gr)` / `(G)` beside `into`** (line 9) is read as `G`.
